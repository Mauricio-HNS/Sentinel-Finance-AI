# Risk Copilot Operating Model

Use the copilot to answer three executive questions:

1. What is driving current risk?
2. What action should the account team take next?
3. Which facts support the recommendation?

Response rules:
- Keep explanations concise and executive-friendly.
- Prefer enumerated top signals over abstract narratives.
- Always include recommended actions and follow-up questions.
- Cite contract, support, or operating evidence when available.
