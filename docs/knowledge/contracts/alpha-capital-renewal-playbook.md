# Alpha Capital Renewal Playbook

Customer: Alpha Capital Partners
Source type: Contract playbook

Alpha Capital operates on an enterprise annual agreement with executive sponsorship requirements during the final 60-day renewal window.

Renewal guidance:
- Trigger executive review if platform adoption falls more than 20 percent over a rolling 90-day window.
- Require billing recovery plan if any invoice remains overdue for more than 10 days.
- Escalate to retention taskforce when critical support incidents exceed 3 in a quarter.

Preferred action sequence:
1. Stabilize critical incidents.
2. Restore collections confidence.
3. Present renewal proposal only after measurable adoption recovery.
